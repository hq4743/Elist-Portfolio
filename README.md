# Elist-Portfolio
Founded in 2018, Elist is a global e-commerce company specializing in consumer electronics, operating across both web and mobile platforms. The company reaches customers through a diversified marketing mix, including email campaigns, search engine optimization (SEO), and affiliate partnerships. Over time, Elist has scaled its product offerings, with strong demand for leading brands such as Apple, Samsung, and ThinkPad.

The analysis uncovers actionable insights into Elist’s revenue drivers, customer purchasing patterns, and geographic performance, identifying areas of over- and under-performance across the business. These findings inform decisions across operations, marketing, and sales. These insights were derived from Elist’s core datasets spanning orders, customers, products, and geographic information.

Insights and recommendations provided based on the following key metrics :

**Average Order Value** : The AOV view shows clear variation across product categories, with premium products consistently driving higher order values compared to lower-priced items. Repeat customers also tend to place higher-value orders than first-time buyers, indicating a positive relationship between customer tenure and spend.

**Overall Sales Trends**:Sales trends indicate overall growth with noticeable fluctuations over time, suggesting seasonality and campaign-driven spikes. 

**Loyalty Members** :**Customers engaged through loyalty or marketing channels demonstrate stronger purchasing behavior compared to non-engaged customers, including higher order frequency and more consistent repeat purchases.

**Apple Product Performance** : Apple products consistently outperform other brands in revenue contribution despite representing a smaller share of total orders.

Together, these insights highlight opportunities to grow revenue through a combination of premium product focus, customer retention strategies, and diversified product performance, rather than relying solely on volume-driven growth.

**Insights and Recommendations **
1. 🌍 USD Price by Region & Revenue by Region
What the Data Shows

-EMEA is Elist's highest-revenue region, generating concentrated sales across Europe and the Middle East. The revenue trend line for EMEA peaks sharply at ~$5K around month 11, indicating a strong holiday/Q4 seasonal surge.
APAC clusters around Australia and Southeast Asia with $193,979 in regional USD price — the highest single geographic concentration on the map, suggesting a loyal customer base in that area.
LATAM contributes $114,707 but shows a flat, low-variance trend line across all months — revenue exists but lacks growth momentum.
NA shows a surprisingly low figure on the map ($1,969) which may reflect a filtered view or currency segmentation in the dataset rather than true NA underperformance.
All four regions show correlated spikes around months 10–12, confirming a global Q4 seasonality pattern driven likely by holiday campaigns.

📌 **Recommendations**

-Double down on EMEA Q4 campaigns — the spike at month 11 confirms this region responds strongly to seasonal promotions. Allocate higher ad spend in October–November for EMEA specifically.
Investigate APAC more granularly — the high USD concentration in Australia/SEA warrants a country-level breakdown. If Australia is driving most APAC revenue, consider a localized storefront or currency option (AUD) to reduce friction.
Activate LATAM with targeted campaigns — flat revenue despite existing customers suggests low marketing penetration, not low demand. Test localized email or affiliate campaigns in Brazil and Mexico.
Validate NA data — if the low NA figure is a filtering artifact, correct it. If accurate, it signals a serious gap in North America that needs a dedicated growth strategy.


2. 📦 **AOV by Product & Overall Sales by Product**
What the Data Shows

-Macbook Air Laptop holds the highest AOV in the entire catalog — peaking around $1,800 in 2019 — but shows a consistent and steep downward trend, dropping to ~$1,250 by 2023. This is a significant erosion of premium value over time.
ThinkPad Laptop maintains a stable AOV of ~$1,100 across the full period — the most consistent premium performer with no meaningful decline.

-Apple iPhone AOV sits around $700–800, relatively flat, with minor fluctuations — reliable but not growing.
27in 4K Gaming Monitor holds steady at ~$400 AOV — lower price point but consistent, suggesting a loyal repeat-purchase segment.
Samsung Charging Cable Pack and Samsung Webcam have near-zero AOV contribution — they are volume accessories, not revenue drivers.
Overall Sales peaked sharply in 2020–2021 across nearly all products — consistent with the global pandemic-driven electronics demand surge. By 2022–2023, revenue dropped significantly across the board, with the 27in Monitor and Apple AirPods seeing the steepest declines.
